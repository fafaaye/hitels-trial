Frequently asked questions

What exactly does Hitels do?

We provide two core services for hotels: custom website design and a direct booking engine. You can use either service individually, or combine both.

Do I have to use both the website and the booking engine?

No. Our services are completely modular. We can build you a new website, integrate our booking engine into your current site, or do both.

How is the booking engine implemented?

We handle the entire technical setup for you. We seamlessly integrate the engine into either your existing website or a new custom site we build.

How is your platform different from Wordpress or Squarespace?

We don’t use DIY templates. We offer a premium, “done-for-you” service, custom-building your site in Framer for superior design, speed, and SEO.

Who is this best for?

Independent and boutique hotels looking to elevate their brand, increase direct bookings, and stop paying high commissions to OTAs.
